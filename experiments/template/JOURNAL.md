# Experiment journal

Use this optional, human-authored capsule for decisions that are not already in
`config.yaml` or the generated records catalog.

## Purpose

Describe the biological or analytical question in one or two sentences.

## Source and replicate semantics

Record the source identity, staged input digest, replicate kind, and stable
replicate identifier when known.

## Decisions and limits

Record consequential interpretation choices, deviations from the protocol, and
known evidence limits.

## Handoff status

State what is ready for review and what remains unresolved. Keep this capsule
human-authored; do not paste command transcripts here.
